  • Importance of PAN Card in Credit Report

  • ✓ Permanent Account Number (PAN) is a 10-digit unique identification alphanumeric number assigned to tax-paying entities in India.
  • ✓ It is mandatory to have a valid and original PAN card to check your free credit score and download your credit report.
  • ✓ As your PAN card is associated with all the financial and banking accounts, it helps Credit Information Companies (CICs) or credit bureaus to easily fetch your details and generate or refresh your credit score.
  • ✓ Lenders request your credit score from these CICs, which help them in assessing the risk of lending you the credit.
  • ✓ While you enter your PAN card to check your credit report or CIBIL score, it is only used to check details of your credit products, such as loans and credit cards and is not used for any other purpose(s).

  • Effect of PAN Card Change on your CIBIL Score

  • Issuance of a Duplicate PAN Card

  • If your PAN card is lost or stolen and you have requested a duplicate PAN card, it won’t have any effect on your CIBIL score.

  • Your PAN will remain the same, and even the past details shall remain identical.

  • In case of lost PAN card, you can also get an ePAN card issued by either NSDL or UTIITSL.

  • If you have two different PAN cards, then it is advisable to surrender one to avoid penal actions in future.

  • Read More:How to Surrender PAN card

  • Issuance of a New PAN Card

  • All of your financial transactions are mapped against your PAN.

  • Your PAN is a unique number and is issued to you for lifetime.

  • Ideally, you should have only one PAN against which all of your financial transactions should be mapped.

  • In case you already have a PAN, you should not apply for a new one.

  • Even if you lose your card, you should apply for a duplicate copy of your PAN.

  • If there’s another PAN issued to you inadvertently, you should surrender it at the earliest and link your Aadhaar with the primary PAN.

  • If you have not linked your PAN with Aadhaar yet, find out how to link PAN and Aadhaar.

  • Factors that affect Your CIBIL Score

  • Following are the 4 major factors that negatively impact your CIBIL Score:

  • 1. Irresponsible Payment Behaviour

  • ✓ Any delayed, missed or non-payment of your loan EMIs or credit card dues will negatively impact your CIBIL score.
  • ✓ Credit repayment history acts as the most crucial component considered by lenders in the credit approval process.
  • ✓ As soon as there is a default, it is marked against your PAN and is shared with the credit bureau by the lender.
  • ✓ The credit bureau adds this information against your PAN and generates the updated credit report.

  • 2. Submitting Multiple Credit Applications

  • ✓ When you apply for a credit product, your PAN is mentioned in the application.
  • ✓ The financial institution fetches your credit report from the bureau to assess your credit behaviour and is termed a hard inquiry.
  • ✓ If you have recently applied for multiple loans or credit cards simultaneously or in a short duration, then the lenders may consider the approval with caution.

  • Suggested Read:Pros and Cons of Owning Multiple Credit Cards

  • 3. High Credit Utilisation Ratio (CUR)

  • ✓ Having a high credit utilisation ratio or maxing out on your credit card frequently displays you to be overdependent on credit, resulting in a high repayment burden.
  • ✓ This again may hamper your CIBIL score and diminish the chances for further loan approval.
  • ✓ Experts suggest keeping your credit utilisation ratio less than 30% of the total sanctioned credit limit.
  • ✓ This shows that your hunger for credit is low, and the chances of you defaulting are less.

  • 4. Diverse Credit Mix

  • ✓ Maintaining a balanced mix between a secured loan (home loan/auto loan) and an unsecured loan (personal loan/credit card/business loan) is likely to have a positive effect on your credit score.
  • ✓ A better credit mix shows that you have handled different types of credit products prudently in the past and thus, the chances of you defaulting in future are relatively lower than those who have not handled multiple credit products in the past.
  • ✓ No lender, however, shall reject your application only because you have not handled multiple credit products in the past.

    FAQs

    Will my CIBIL Score be affected when I update my PAN card? +
    No, your CIBIL score will not be affected when you update your PAN card. The PAN would remain the same, and there would be a change only in details in the PAN without impacting your credit score.
    Is there a fee associated with checking the CIBIL Score using a PAN Card? +
    When you check your CIBIL score at TransUnion CIBIL’s official website, it is free of cost only once a year, and for the rest, you will have to purchase a subscription plan. However, checking your CIBIL score is free of cost at Paisabazaar, and you get monthly updates for free for lifetime as well.
    Will my signature change on the PAN Card impact my credit Score? +
    Ideally, you should not change your signature as it may lead to unwanted circumstances in future, especially related to your finances. However, your changed signature, if updated in your PAN card, may not have an impact on your credit score.
    What should I do if there's an error in my CIBIL report obtained using PAN Card? +
    If you find any minor or major errors, mismatched details, or inaccuracies in your CIBIL report fetched by PAN card, you need to raise a dispute with TransUnion CIBIL. You can submit an online dispute resolution form and get errors resolved at the earliest.
    Does applying for a new PAN Card impact your CIBIL Score? +
    Your CIBIL score is calculated based on the details of loans or credit cards linked to your existing PAN card. If you already have a PAN card, you are not required to apply for a new one, as owning more than one PAN card is a punishable offence and the Income Tax department may take action against you.
    Can CIBIL Score be checking by PAN CArd without OTP? +
    As the CIBIL Score is considered personal information, an OTP is required to verify your authenticity at the time of checking the credit score. A customer's personal information is foremost at Paisabazaar, and thus, it is not possible to check the CIBIL score by PAN card without OTP.
    What are the effect of PAN change on my CIBIL Score? +
    Your CIBIL score is calculated based on the credit products linked to your PAN card. In case a different PAN card is mentioned in your CIBIL report, your credit score may vary. However, it is recommended that you get it corrected as early as possible, otherwise, lowering your credit score due to incorrect information in times of need can make it difficult for you to get your credit application approved.
    What is a newly issued PAN Card's CIBIL SCore? +
    PAN card is required to apply for a loan or credit card. When you get your PAN card for the first time and you haven’t applied for a loan, credit bureaus do not have any information regarding your credit history. Hence, they mark ‘New to Credit’ or ‘No History’ against your profile. It, however, does not mean that lenders will necessarily deny you a loan or credit card if you fulfil other criteria.
    Does SBI use my PAN card to check the CIBIL Score for personal loans? +
    When you apply for an SBI personal loan, the bank requests your credit score from a credit bureau, probably CIBIL. Your PAN is required to generate your CIBIL report, as all existing and old credit details are linked to your PAN. The loan approval depends largely on your CIBIL report, apart from various other eligibility criteria.
    Can I Check my Credit Score if I do not have a PAN Card? +
    Your major financial transactions are recorded against your PAN card. If you don’t have a PAN card, there is a high probability that you haven’t availed of any credit. In such a case, no credit bureau will be able to generate your credit score.
    Can I generate my Credit Score using an Aadhar Card? +
    Even though you have to link your PAN card with Aadhaar, as of now, you cannot get your credit score using your Aadhaar number only. You will have to provide your PAN to generate your credit score.
    How to check free CIBIL Score bt PAN number? +
    You can check CIBIL score by PAN card number by visiting Paisabazaar’s website. You just have to provide details such as PAN, mobile number, name, gender, date of birth and OTP the first time. From next time, you can check free CIBIL score by PAN number by just providing your mobile number and OTP.
