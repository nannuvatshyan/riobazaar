Top Cashback Credit Cards in India 2026

Here are the top credit cards in India that reward you with decent cashback for your expenses.

  • 1.YES Bank Paisabazaar PaisaSave Credit Card

  • Best for: Dining & Travel
  • Joining Fee: Nil
  • Annual Fee: Rs. 499 + Taxes (Waived off on spending Rs. 1.2 lakh in a year)

  • Key Features and Benefits:

  • ✓ 6% cashback on dining & travel spends
  • ✓ 1% cashback on other categories & UPI spends
  • ✓ 1% fuel surcharge waiver across all petrol pumps, up to Rs. 250 per month
  • ✓ Annual fee waiver on spending Rs. 1.2 Lakh in a year
    • Offering 6% cashback on all dining & travel spends without any merchant restrictions, YES BANK Paisabazaar PaisaSave Credit Card turns out to be one of the best cashback credit cards for travel and dining. While most travel credit cards offer value-back through Air miles and dining cards usually come with co-branded benefits, this card stands out due to its no brand restrictions. Though the 6% cashback is capped at Rs. 3,000 per month, cardholders can still save significantly on their monthly dining and travel expenses. Besides this, it also offers 1% base cashback on other categories, including UPI spends. Overall, this card is suitable for consumers who want a simple, high-value cashback card to save on all dining and travel spends without any merchant restrictions.

  • 2.Cashback SBI Card

  • Best for: Online Shopping
  • Joining Fee: Rs. 999
  • Annual Fee:Rs. 999 (Waived off on spending Rs. 2 lakh in a year)

  • Key Features and Benefits:

  • ✓ 5% cashback on online spends
  • ✓ 1% cashback only on offline transactions
  • ✓ Annual fee reversal on spending Rs. 2 Lakh in a year
  • ✓ 1% fuel surcharge waiver across all petrol pumps in India
    • Cashback SBI Card is another option for online shoppers as it gives 5% flat cashback across all online purchases irrespective of the merchant or platform. The card is best suited for consumers who prefer shopping through e-commerce platforms but do not want to limit the benefits to one or more brands or categories. Cashback on online and offline spends (combined) is limited to Rs. 5,000 in a month, after which no cashback can be earned on the card. Despite the capping, the card remains a good option for high-spending online shoppers who can easily maximize its benefits.

However, there is minimum transaction limit to be eligible for the cashback, and maximum capping on the cashback that you can earn in a billing cycle. Hence, this card would be a great choice for high-spenders who prefer the associated brands.

Hence, the card covers 3 very popular categories – shopping, travel, and lifestyle. Similar cards like Amazon Pay and Paytm SBI Card offer great benefits on their own respective platforms and some partner merchants. However, the partner merchants on Flipkart Card are among the most popular ones in their categories, making the card more practical. Flipkart loyalists, who also seek benefits with other preferred merchants can consider getting this card.

Unlike most cards that generally exclude EMI transactions from their rewards program, this card offers 1.5% value-back across all purchases made outside the co-branded ecosystem, including EMI transactions. The card can be a good choice for consumers who often shop with the eligible Tata Group Brands and can easily maximize the benefits.

    How did we pick these cashback credit cards?


    When selecting credit cards for this list, we have evaluated the cards based on the following factors:

    Value-Back Across Categories: We prioritized cards that provide maximum value-back across a wide variety of spends, say online shopping, and then moved on to cards offering category-specific or brand-specific benefits. Along with this, base rate offered by the card across a wide range of categories was also considered.

    Reward Rates & Limits: The cashback percentage, along with any applicable caps or limits, played a significant role in our decision-making. Cards with higher cashback rates, generous limits, and fewer restrictions were given preference.

    Co-Branded Benefits: Many of the cards are co-branded with popular online and offline platforms and merchants. We focused on partnerships that bring real, everyday value to users while also ensuring that the cards provide strong returns on spends made outside of their co-branded networks.

    Fees vs. Benefits: We weighed the annual fees associated with each card against the potential rewards and prioritized cards that justify their annual fees through benefits.

    How to choose the right cashback credit card?


    The choice of best credit card for cashback depends on how you plan to use the card. Since credit card benefits are usually inclined towards one or two specific categories, understanding your need is very important while choosing any type of card. For example, if you wish to save on your Amazon Spends, then Amazon Pay ICICI Credit Card would be a good choice for you. However if you plan to use the card across multiple online platforms, then a card that gives good value across all online spends like YES Bank Paisabazaar PaisaSave Credit Card would be better.

    Once you have narrowed down your options, you should compare the credit cards on the basis of annual fee, cashback rate, bonus categories, etc.

    FAQs

    What is the best Cashback credit card for offline spends? +
    Some of the best credit cards for offline spends are YES Bank Paisabazaar PaisaSave Credit Card (1.5%), Axis Ace Credit Card (1.5%), HSBC Live+ Credit Card (1.5%) and Myntra Kotak Credit Card (1.25%).
    Which credit card gives maximum cashback in india? +
    Currently, the credit cards offering maximum cashback are:
    Airtel Axis Credit Card: 25% cashback on payment for Airtel Mobile, DTH, etc. via Airtel Thanks App
    Swiggy HDFC Credit Card: 10% cashback on Swiggy food orders, Instamart, Dineout & Genie
    HSBC Live+: 10% cashback (up to Rs. 1,000/month) on dining, grocery & food delivery
    However, it is important to note that all of these cards offer highest cashback on select categories. You should get a card that offers good cashback on your preferred spends.
    Which is the best cashback credit card utility bill payments? +
    Some of the best credit cards for utility bill payment are Airtel Axis, Axis Ace, Swiggy HDFC and Standard Chartered Super Value Titanium. Note that on each of these cards, the earnings on utility bill payments are capped. Hence, you should spend accordingly.
    Which RuPay credit card is best for cashback? +
    Some of the best Rupay Credit Cards for cashback are Tata Neu Infinity HDFC Bank Credit Card, Tata Neu Plus HDFC Bank Credit Card, YES Bank Paisabazaar PaisaSave Credit Card and Myntra Kotak Credit Card.
    Which is better-Cashback or rewards? +
    The idea behind cashback and rewards is the same- they are simply an incentive for using your card. While cashback is simple to understand and manage, reward points are a bit complicated because of the extra step of first accumulating them and then redeeming them at a later stage. Some cards allow you to convert the reward points into cash but the rate may not be the same. At the same time, there are rewards credit cards that allow conversion into miles which could be better than cashback, especially if you travel often. So, you must make a choice depending on your needs
    Is a cashback credit card worth it?? +
    Any credit card would be worth only if you manage to get benefits at least equivalent to the annual fee charged. Most cashback cards charge a low annual fee so it is likely that you would be able to make the most of it even if you do not put every expense on the card. However, you should always pay your bills on time so that the extra charges do not eat into your benefits.
    How to maximize cashback on your credit card? +
    The easiest way to maximize the benefits on your cashback credit card is to use it for the bonus category of expenses. For example, if your cashback credit card offers fuel benefits, you must put your fuel expenses on this card to make the most of it.
    I already have a credit card. Should I get a cashback credit card too? +
    You should consider getting a cashback credit card if your rewards card does not cover a particular spending category. Even if you are happy with your rewards credit card, getting a low or no annual fee cashback credit card does not hurt.
    Is 2% cashback good? +
    Yes. This is because rewards may look like a lot but their actual value back is low. Mostly, the monetary value of 1 reward point ranges from Re. 0.25 to Re. 0.50. But when you get real and direct cashback, even 2% is better.
    What are the typical cashback percentages? +
    Most cashback credit cards offer base rate of 1% to 1.5% across all spends along with higher cashback on select categories or brands. For example, Amazon Pay ICICI Credit Card gives 5% cashback on Amazon Spends for Prime Members and 3% for Non-Prime members. However, spends outside Amazon earn only 1% cashback.
    Do cashback credit cards have an annual fee? +
    Yes. Most cashback credit cards come with a joining and annual fee. Depending on the card, annual fees can vary from Rs. 499 to Rs. 1,499 or higher. However, it is advisable not to make annual fee the primary criterion while choosing a cashback credit card. Check the card’s features and benefits and see if it aligns with your spending habits.
