    Benefits of Fuel Credit Cards


    With different card issuers, the benefits offered on fuel credit cards differ. Some fuel cards offer direct discount on fuel transactions while others offer value-back through rewards. Here are some of the common benefits that come with the best fuel credit cards in India:

    Cashback/ Reward Points:
    Fuel credit cards often offer higher rewards or cashback on your fuel transactions at partner fuel outlets. Some cards also offer bonus rewards as brand loyalty points for fuel purchases at select outlets or on payment via co-branded fuel app...

    Redemption Options:
    Another way to enhance your savings is through reward points redemption. Fuel credit cards not only offer higher rewards on your fuel expenses but also allow you to redeem the earned reward points against future fuel purchases. For instance, with IndianOil HDFC Credit Card, you can get free fuel of up to 50 litres every year by earning and redeeming the reward points. These accrued reward points can also be redeemed against other options, such as statement credit, gift vouchers and much more.

    Co-branded Benefits:
    Most of the fuel credit cards are co-branded, which means they offer higher benefits only on the collaborated brands. For instance, BPCL SBI Card Octane gives you a value-back of up to 7.25% for your purchases at BPCL outlets. Hence, it is important to get a credit card that offers higher rewards or cashback at the fuel stations you prefer visiting.

    Complimentary Benefits:
    Besides basic benefits, some of the cards also offer complimentary benefits like memberships, bonus rewards on achieving spending milestones and more. For instance, IndianOil HDFC Credit Card comes with a complimentary IndianOil XTRAREWARDSTM Program (IXRP) membership, allowing you to enhance your savings on IndiaOil outlets

    How to Choose the Best Fuel Credit Card?

  • Here are a few factors that you should look for in a best credit card for fuel in India that best aligns with your needs:

  • Co-branded Benefits You should consider co-branded benefits offered by the card. For instance, if you frequently visit HPCL petrol pumps, then you should opt for a card that offers higher benefits on your fuel transactions made at HPCL petrol pumps rather than having a card that offers similar benefits on other fuel brands.
    Additional Discount You should look for a card that lets you save more on your fuel transactions. Some cards offer an additional discount on your fuel transactions at the collaborated brand. Some credit cards offer an additional discount when you achieve a certain spending milestone, whereas, some cards offer an additional discount for a specific period of time at the partnered petrol pump.
    Higher Rewards/ Cashback You should get a credit card that offers higher rewards or cashback on fuel transactions. For instance, IndianOil Axis Bank Credit Card rewards you with a 1% value-back for your online transactions, whereas, when it comes to fuel expenses, this credit card offers a value-back of 4%, which is higher as compared to other categories.
    Fuel Surcharge Waiver The fuel surcharge is a charge that is levied on your fuel transactions across all the petrol pumps. Fuel credit cards let you save on this surcharge by providing a waiver on your transactions. This is a basic benefit that is offered by most of the credit cards, but varies in terms of the eligible transactions and the maximum amount that you can get waived off every month.

    FAQs

    What are the benefits offered on fuel credit cards in India? +
    Most of the fuel credit cards are co-branded and offer benefits on the collaborated brand. With fuel cards, you can earn discounts on your fuel expenses or you can also earn cashback or rewards for your transactions. Most of the best fuel cards also allow you to redeem the earned rewards against future fuel purchases, allowing you to get free fuel. Besides these benefits, fuel credit cards also offer complimentary benefits, like exclusive memberships and bonus rewards.
    How can I apply for the best fuel credit card in India? +
    Fuel credit cards are offered by most of the leading credit card issuers, including HDFC, Axis, Kotak, ICICI, SBI Card and more.You can apply for any of the fuel credit cards by visiting the official website of the respective card issuer or by visiting any of the nearest bank branches. Besides this, you can also click here to check your eligibility & apply for fuel credit cards through Paisabazaar.
    Are fuel credit cards worth it? +
    If you commute on a daily basis and fuel expenses cover a large portion of your monthly expenditures, then having a fuel credit card is a good option for you.Some fuel cards offer value-back via cashback or rewards while others offer direct discount. With the right card, you can save on current fuel expenses and also earn additional benefits like bonus rewards which can help you fetch free fuel every year.
    What are the best lifetime free fuel credit cards in India? +
    Most of the card issuers offer lifetime free credit cards which allows you to save on fuel surcharge. But, at present, fuel credit cards are offered with an annual fee as low as Rs. 199. Before applying for any of the fuel credit cards, you must analyse your spending pattern and choose the card that aligns the best with your monthly expenses.
    How can I perform fuel credit cards comparison? +
    Before selecting the right fuel card, it is important to compare cards from different issuers to make an informed decision. You can click here to check your eligibility and compare different fuel credit cards in India.
    Which credit card for fuel is the best in India? +
    Currently, BPCL SBI Card Octane is one of the best fuel credit cards available in the market if you are a loyal customer of BPCL. With this card, you can earn 25X rewards on your fuel expenses and get a value-back of up to 7.25%.You can also apply for other fuel credit cards like IndianOil Axis or ICICI HPCL Super Saver if you prefer other fuel outlets over BPCL. But the best card can vary for each individual depending upon your personal spending patterns, needs and spending capacity.
    What is a fuel surcharge waiver on credit card? +
    Every time you make a fuel transaction through your credit card, a fuel surcharge is levied which is around 1% to 2% of your transaction amount. However, most of the credit cards offer a fuel surcharge waiver of 1% on your fuel transactions.
    Is there an eligibility criterion to apply for a fuel credit card? +
    The eligibility criteria for a fuel credit card vary from issuer to issuer. However, you must have a regular source of income and must be above 18 years old to avail a credit card. You should check the eligibility for the credit card before applying for one to avoid credit card application rejection.
    What are the disadvantages of fuel cards in India? +
    Most of the fuel credit cards are co-branded with certain fuel providers which makes them beneficial only if you prefer to make fuel purchases from the respective brand which can be restrictive for some card users.Moreover, most of these cards offer reward points, so if you prefer to get direct cashback then your options can be limited. Also, if you want better value-back on other spending categories, like online shopping, travel, dining, etc., then opting for other card types would be better.
    Which is the best HP fuel credit card? +
    If you prefer HPCL over other fuel outlets, you can apply for ICICI HPCL Super Saver Credit Card, ICICI HPCL Coral Credit Card or HPCL IDFC FIRST Power+ Credit Card. With these cards, you can get up to 6.5% back on your fuel expenses through rewards or cashback. Remember, all these credit cards charge an annual fee and you should analyse the overall value-back that you can get via the card to make an informed decision.
    How to avoid fuel surcharge on credit cards? +
    You can avoid 1% fuel surcharge by using a fuel credit card. Besides fuel-specific credit cards, some general credit cards also offer 1% fuel surcharge waiver.However, this benefit is usually applicable only for transactions within a specific amount range and comes with a maximum capping. For example, with BPCL SBI Card Octane, you can avail 1% fuel surcharge waiver, capped at Rs. 100 per month, on fuel transactions up to Rs. 4,000.
    How can I earn 50 liters of free fuel annually with a fuel credit card? +
    With IndianOil HDFC Credit Card, you can earn up to 5% of your fuel spends at IndianOil fuel stations as Fuel Points and save significantly on fuel expenses.According to the bank, these Fuel Points can be redeemed against your fuel purchases at IndianOil fuel stations, allowing you to earn up to 50 litres of free fuel annually.
    Is it good to pay for fuel using a credit card? +
    Yes, paying for fuel with the right credit card can help you save money and even earn free fuel every year. Fuel credit cards typically offer reward points or cashback on fuel transactions, which you can accumulate and redeem against fuel purchases at partnered brands. For example, with cards like IndianOil RBL XTRA and BPCL SBI Card Octane, you can save up to 8.5% on fuel expenses.
    What are the disadvantages of a fuel credit card? +
    Most fuel credit cards are co-branded with specific fuel companies, such as IndianOil, Bharat Petroleum, and HPCL. As a result, these cards offer higher rewards only at the partnered brand’s fuel stations. They primarily cater to fuel purchases and do not offer significant benefits across other spending categories. Hence, while great for fuel expenses, they may not be ideal as all-rounder credit cards.
    What should I consider when choosing a fuel credit card? +
    Since most fuel credit cards are co-branded, you should first identify your preferred fuel brand, as these cards offer accelerated benefits only at partnered stations. Additionally, consider the overall value of the card—check if it offers benefits beyond fuel, such as dining, shopping, or travel, to ensure it aligns with your spending habits.
    How can I maximize rewards with a fuel credit card? +
    To maximize your savings with a fuel credit card, always use it at the partnered fuel brand’s outlets to earn accelerated rewards. Besides this, keep track of accumulated reward points and redeem them against fuel purchases to get free fuel.
