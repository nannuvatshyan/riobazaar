Best Credit Cards for International Travel 2026

With the right travel credit card, cardholders can save significantly on their travel spends through complimentary memberships, accelerated rewards and more. While most of the travel cards offer accelerated benefits on travel spends, only premium credit cards offer benefits like unlimited lounge visits, low forex markup fees and decent value-back through reward redemption for travel spends.     Benefits of Using International Travel Credit Card


    Here are some of the benefits that you can avail with best credit cards for international travel:

    Low Forex Markup Fee
    Good credit cards for international travel often charge a low forex markup fee as compared to other cards, typically ranging between 0% to 2%, comparatively lower than the standard rate of 3.5%. With the right credit card, you can significantly enhance your savings by taking advantage of lower forex markup fee. For instance, RBL World Safari charges a forex markup fee of 0%, making it a great option for international use.

    Higher Rewards on International Transactions

    Certain credit cards offer higher rewards specifically tailored for international transactions, a feature typically absent in standard credit cards. Take, for instance, AU Zenith+ Card, which offers 2X points on travel and international spends, along with 1 reward point for every Rs. 100 spent on other categories. Opting for a card that offers higher rewards for international transactions can significantly elevate your travel experience and maximize reward benefits.

    Complimentary Lounge Access

    Complimentary lounge access is a benefit that is offered not only on travel-specific cards but also on general cards. However, travel credit cards typically offer a higher frequency or unlimited lounge visits for primary and add-on users. The number of visits varies from card to card, and usually, only premium cards with high annual fees offer unlimited lounge access. Frequent travellers can save significantly on their travels through these benefits.

    Additional Travel Perks

    In addition to the low forex markup fee and reward benefits, top international travel credit cards also allow you to avail additional perks like free memberships, complimentary holiday vouchers, airport pickups, travel assistance, and much more to enhance your overall travel experience. While these benefits might not solely decide the choice of a travel card, they undeniably allow you to enhance your savings and the overall value of the card.

    FAQs

    Which is the best credit card for international travel? +
    There are numerous cards available in the market that are suitable for international travel. While any credit card allows international transactions, certain ones offer lower forex markup fees or extra perks for such transactions, enabling savings during shopping abroad. Some of the best credit cards for international travel are IDFC First Wealth, HDFC Diners Club Privilege, SBI Aurum, and more. The selection among these cards entirely depends on your spending habits and travel preferences.
    What features should I look for in a credit card for international travel? +
    If you are looking for a credit card for international travel, you should consider a card that is accepted internationally, and offers low forex mark-up fee and complimentary international lounge access. Some credit cards also provide accelerated value-back on international spends and could be a good option for you if you frequently travel overseas or make purchases in foreign currency. Moreover, cards with additional benefits like emergency replacement in case of loss, emergency overseas health insurance cover, round the clock assistance, etc. could also make your international trips hassle-free.
    What is the best new credit card for international travel? +
    Among the newly launched credit cards in India, HSBC Taj, Times Black ICICI Credit Card and MakeMyTrip ICICI Credit Card are some of the good options for international travel.
    Which is the most accepted credit card internationally? +
    Among all payment networks, Visa and Mastercard have the widest international acceptance. While Rupay, Diners Club and American Express are also accepted at most international locations, they may have limited reach in some areas.
    What is the best way to pay for things abroad? +
    Carrying large amounts of cash during your international travel is not safe. Hence, card-based payment options are the best. These include forex cards and credit cards. Forex cards are prepaid cards that are pre-loaded in one or more foreign currencies of your choice. When you make a payment in the pre-loaded currency, you will not be charged any additional fee. Similarly, credit cards that come with a low or zero foreign exchange mark-up fee could make international transactions convenient for you, at minimal costs. With some credit cards, you also get additional travel benefits like complimentary lounge access, airport meet and greet, hotel memberships, etc. which could further add value for travellers.
    Which credit cards are free for international transactions? +
    You can make international transactions using almost all credit cards. However, most of them charge a forex markup fee of around 3.5%. Only a few premium credit cards that are designed for international transactions charge zero or low forex markup fees. Therefore, if you travel internationally, it’s better to choose a card with a low or zero forex markup fee.
    How can I apply for a credit card for international travel? +
    Major credit card issuers offer international credit cards. You can compare & apply for a credit card international travel online by visiting the respective card issuer’s official website or offline by visiting the nearest branch of the bank. However, before applying for a card, you must check the eligibility requirements and other terms & conditions to avoid credit card application rejection.
    I am a Non-Resident Indian (NRI). Can I apply for a credit card for international from a bank in India? +
    Yes, most of the banks allow you to apply for a credit card for international travel even if you are an NRI. However, the eligibility criteria for NRIs may vary from those applicable to Indian residents.The approval of your card application is completely at the discretion of the bank.
    Which is the best credit card for international lounge access? +
    The majority of credit cards provide international lounge access through Priority Pass Membership. Yet, for frequent travellers, having a credit card that offers a higher number of international lounge visits can be beneficial. In these instances, super-premium cards like ICICI Emeralde, HDFC Diners Club Black, and others offer unlimited international lounge access annually.
    Is there a limit on the amount of purchases made in foreign currencies? +
    There is no separate credit limit on credit cards that is specifically assigned for foreign currency transactions. Your overall credit limit, covering both domestic and foreign currency transactions can be used for international transactions. However, to avoid unauthorised usage, you can set a limit on your credit card for international spends through net banking or mobile banking.
