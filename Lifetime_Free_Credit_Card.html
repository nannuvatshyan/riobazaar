    Things you should know before choosing a lifetime free credit card +

    Before choosing a lifetime free credit card, two major points to consider are:

  • Check other fees & charges: Though the annual and joining fees are waived, other fees and charges are also applicable on your credit cards. For instance, someone who frequently travels abroad should check if the card charges a low forex markup fee

  • Choose the right credit card:Though a lifetime free credit card seems cost-effective, for long-run benefits, it is better to choose a card that matches the best with your lifestyle preferences and spending habits. Always, compare multiple card options, and go for a better card if you can afford to pay

  • While the features and benefits of lifetime free credit cards vary basis the card variant, here are some of the common benefits that you can avail from such cards:

  • No Joining/Annual Fee


  • Since lifetime free credit cards come with no joining or annual fees, they are a cost-effective option for individuals who want the convenience of credit without the additional financial burden.

  • Credit Building


  • Lifetime free credit cards are a great tool for building your credit score. By using the card responsibly, paying bills on time, and keeping credit utilization low, you can gradually improve your credit score. This can make it easier for you to qualify for regular or premium credit cards in the future.

  • Relaxed Eligibility Criteria


  • Since most lifetime free credit cards are entry-level cards, they might come with easier eligibility terms. These cards are usually targeted towards first-time credit card users or salaried professionals with moderate incomes. This makes it easier to qualify for the card without stringent income or credit score requirements.

  • Rewards or Cashback Benefits


  • Despite being free, lifetime free credit cards offer rewards or cashback on transactions, such as purchases on groceries, dining, or fuel. You can accumulate reward points or earn cashback, which can later be redeemed for discounts, vouchers, or statement credits. This adds extra value to your everyday spending.

    FAQ +

  • Which banks offer lifetime free credit cards?
    Popular card issuers in India, such as ICICI Bank, IDFC FIRST Bank, HSBC Bank, etc., offer lifetime free credit cards. Some other issuers may also run no annual fee credit card offers from time to time. You must track the issuers’s website for updated details to get one such card.

  • Can I use a lifetime free credit card overseas?Yes, most lifetime free credit cards come with international acceptance and can be used overseas. However, a foreign exchange markup fee of up to 3.5% may be charged on purchases made in foreign currency, depending on the card variant.

  • Is a ‘lifetime free’ credit card truly free, or are there hidden charges?A life time free credit card does not charge any joining or annual fee ever. However, charges like late payment fee, cash advance fee, interest, etc., will be applicable depending on how you use the card.

  • Does Kotak Mahindra Bank offer a lifetime free credit card?Yes, Kotak Mahindra Bank offers a lifetime free credit card- Kotak 811 #DreamDifferent Credit Card. However, it is a secured credit card issued against a fixed deposit with the 90% of the FD amount as credit limit.

  • What happens if I don’t pay the bill on my no annual fee credit card?When you don’t pay the bill by the due date, you will have to incur late payment charges as well as finance charges (interest). The rate varies from card to card. Also, when you have an unpaid balance in your credit card account, the interest-free period also stands cancelled which means all new transactions will also attract finance charges from the first day.

  • What are all the potential fees I need to know about before I get the card?Lifetime free credit cards do not charge a joining fee or annual fee. However, other charges, such as late payment fees and finance charges, will apply. When getting a lifetime free credit card, consumers must confirm with the bank if this benefit comes with any additional terms and conditions, such as meeting a spend threshold.

  • Can I upgrade or downgrade my existing credit card to a lifetime free credit card?If you have a credit card that charges a high annual fee and you are not able to make the most of its benefits to compensate for the fee, it would be better to upgrade/downgrade to a lifetime fee variant. You can explore the available options and speak with a customer support representative to initiate the process. However, approval or rejection will be at the issuer’s sole discretion.
