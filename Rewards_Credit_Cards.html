5 Best Rewards Credit Cards in India 2026

March 3, 2025 Megha Khanna Credit Card


Reward points are considered to be one of the most attractive benefits offered by credit cards. These are credited to your card account when you use your card for making payments and can later be redeemed against multiple options, varying across card type and issuers. Once you reach a certain reward point threshold, for example, 2,000 points, you can redeem the same as gift vouchers, merchandise, partner points, or cash credit.

    Strategies to Optimize Credit Card Rewards Benefits +

  • Here are some of the things you should keep in mind to choose the best rewards credit card:


  • Plan your spendings: You should never spend the money just for the sake of earning rewards. Before using your card, you should analyze the overall benefits and make an informed decision. For instance, if you have a card that offers decent rewards on day-to-day expenses, then you must use it for everyday purchases instead of using it for online shopping.


  • Choose the right credit card: You should choose a card that matches your lifestyle the best. If you love to travel frequently, then choose a card that offers decent rewards on travel expenses. Similarly, if you frequently shop online, then choose a card that offers higher rewards on online transactions.

  • What is the interest rate for credit card dues?
    Credit card interest rates usually range between 30% per annum to 48% per annum. However, this rate may vary from issuer to issuer and card to card


  • Read the fine print: Before applying for a credit card , you must read all the terms and conditions carefully to maximize your benefits. Most of the card issuers keep changing the terms and conditions from time to time. Therefore, you should keep track of such changes by visiting the official website of your card issuer.


  • Do not miss out on offers: The card issuers let you save more by providing exclusive deals or offers, especially during the festive season. These offers might be beneficial for you and can help you make the most of your credit card. But again, be cautious and aware of the applicable terms and conditions.


  • Know your spending limits: Most of the card issuers provide additional or bonus rewards on achieving a certain spending milestone. Keep a check on these benefits as they can help you maximize your savings. However, you should always spend as per your repayment ability and not simply to achieve a milestone perk.


  • Consider welcome benefits: Some credit cards come with exciting welcome benefits, such as bonus rewards, gift vouchers, discounts, etc on activating your card or on paying the joining fee. You should never neglect such kind of benefits. These benefits are offered only once at the time of joining and can help you compensate for the joining fee.
