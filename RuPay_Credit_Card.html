  • About RuPay Credit Cards

  • RuPay Credit Card is issued on the RuPay payment network and works like a regular credit card, allowing you to borrow funds up to a certain limit for purchases and repay later. Similar to Visa and Mastercard, RuPay Credit Cards can be used at POS terminals and on e-commerce platforms while also offering benefits like rewards or cashback.

    What makes RuPay Credit Cards stand out is their integration with UPI, allowing you to avail benefits even on UPI transactions, a feature currently unavailable with credit cards from other networks.

    It is also important to note that while some cards are designed specifically for UPI transactions and offer accelerated rewards, other cards on the RuPay network may only offer base rewards or cashback on UPI spends. Additionally, some card issuers also offer virtual RuPay credit cards for both new and existing cardholders.

    Types of RuPay Credit Cards


    RuPay Credit Cards are offered in multiple variants from entry-level to premium. Here is the difference between types of RuPay credit cards:

    RuPay Classic Credit Card:
    This is an entry-level variant best suited for first-time credit card users. It comes with basic benefits such as rewards or cashback on everyday spends, fuel surcharge waiver, and more..

    RuPay Platinum Credit Card:
    This is a mid-tier variant that offers higher spending limits, better reward rates, and additional benefits on shopping, dining, and travel. It usually comes with a comparatively higher annual fee.

    RuPay Select Credit Card:
    This variant is designed for consumers seeking a premium card with enhanced lifestyle benefits. It offers perks such as higher reward rates, concierge services, lounge access, and more.

    RuPay EKAA Credit Card:
    This is a super-premium variant that offers luxury benefits such as emergency cash assistance, concierge services, complimentary golf games, and exclusive travel privileges.

  • Features and Benefits of RuPay Credit Cards

  • Rewards and Cashback

  • RuPay Credit Cards offer reward points and cashback on a wide range of transactions, including shopping, dining, fuel, travel, and more. However, the value-back varies from card to card. While cashback is usually credited to the statement balance, reward points can be redeemed across multiple categories through the rewards catalogue of the respective card issuer.

  • Benefits on UPI Spends

  • With RuPay Credit Cards now being enabled on UPI, you can make credit card payments directly through UPI apps like PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, etc. This integration allows you to earn rewards and cashback even on UPI transactions, which was earlier not possible with credit cards from other networks. Besides this, some issuers also offer accelerated benefits on UPI spends made via RuPay cards.

  • Building a Credit Profile

  • Like other credit cards, RuPay cards also help you build a strong credit profile. Using a RuPay Credit Card responsibly, like paying your dues on time, staying within your credit limit, and keeping your credit utilization low, can help build and improve your credit score.

  • Secure Transactions

  • RuPay Credit Cards are equipped with EMV chip technology that allows you to make secure, high-value transactions. Backed by NPCI, RuPay cards come with an added layer of security, as RuPay follows stringent policies and comes with real-time fraud monitoring for both online and offline transactions.

  • Wider Reach

  • RuPay Credit Cards are issued by a broad network of public and private sector banks, including SBI Card, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and more. These banks have extensive networks of branches, ATMs, and merchant tie-ups across the country, making it easy for you to use RuPay credit cards across India.

  • How to Link RuPay Credit Card to UPI?

  • Follow the below mentioned steps to link your RuPay Credit Card to UPI:

  • Step 1: Open a UPI-enabled app such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.
    Step 2: Go to the ‘Add Credit Card’ section.
    Step 3: Select your issuer that offers RuPay Credit Cards.
    Step 4: Enter your card details and verify with OTP.
    Step 5: Set a UPI PIN for credit card transactions.
    Step 6: Once linked, you can use a RuPay Credit Card for all UPI-based transactions at supported merchants.

  • Where are RuPay Credit Cards Accepted?

  • RuPay Credit Cards can be used for both card-present transactions, such as those made at ATMs and point-of-sale (POS) terminals, and card-not-present transactions like online shopping, IVR, and more.

  • While domestic RuPay Credit Cards are accepted at all RuPay-enabled retail terminals, online websites, and ATMs across India, international RuPay Credit Cards can be used at all RuPay acceptance points within India as well as at ICS Partner acceptance points globally, including ATMs, retail stores, and e-commerce platforms.

    FAQs

    How can I apply for a RuPay credit card? +
    You can apply for select RuPay Credit Cards online through Paisabazaar. Log in using your mobile number and OTP, and you will be able to see the pre-approved credit card offers available for you. Select the RuPay credit card of your choice and proceed with the application. Alternatively, you can visit the respective bank’s website or branch to apply for a RuPay Credit Card.
    What are the benefits of RuPay credit cards? +
    RuPay credit cards function similarly to standard credit card allowing you to earn benefits on your daily transactions. With RuPay cards, you can earn rewards, cashback, complimentary lounge access, complimentary memberships, gift vouchers, and much more. What sets RuPay credit cards apart from others is their integration with UPI. By linking your RuPay credit card with UPI, you can also earn benefits on your UPI transactions.
    How can I link my RuPay credit card with UPI? +
    Cardholders can link their RuPay credit cards with UPI using a UPI-enabled mobile app. You are required to add your card info under the ‘Payment’ section by entering card details, including card number, CVV, and expiry date. After this, you can use your RuPay credit card for UPI transactions.
    Can RuPay credit cards be used for international transactions? +
    Yes, you can use your RuPay credit cards for international transactions. However, a forex markup will be charged on your foreign currency transactions, which is typically 3.5% of the transacted amount. However, this fee varies from card to card.
    Can I withdraw money from my RuPay credit card? +
    Yes, you can withdraw cash from your RuPay credit card at ATMs, but cash withdrawal usually attracts a fee and interest from the date of withdrawal.
    What cashback or rewards do I get with my RuPay credit card? +
    Rewards and cashback vary by card type; some RuPay credit cards offer cashback on fuel, groceries, or online shopping, while others offer points redeemable against vouchers or other categories.
    What is the eligibility criteria to apply for a RuPay credit card? +
    To apply, you must be an Indian resident, meet the minimum age and income requirements, and have a good credit history as specified by the issuer. However, eligibility criteria vary from card to card, so you should check the eligibility criteria before applying to avoid rejection.
    Is there a lifetime free RuPay credit card? +
    Yes, RuPay, in collaboration with popular issuers such as AU Small Finance Bank, IndusInd Bank, YES BANK, and more, offers lifetime free RuPay credit cards. However, these offers may change from time to time, so it is advisable to check the official website of the respective card issuer for the latest updates.
    Can I convert RuPay Credit Card purchases into EMIs? +
    Yes, you can convert your RuPay credit card purchases into EMIs. You can contact your card issuer and ask them to convert the required transaction into EMI. Besides this, you can also do the same by logging into your mobile banking or net banking portal of the respective issuer.
    Which RuPay credit card is best for UPI transactions? +
    If you want a RuPay credit card with a minimal annual fee, you can apply for HDFC Bank UPI RuPay Credit Card. However, if you prefer brand-specific benefits, you can compare & apply for co-branded credit cards, such as Tata Neu HDFC, IndianOil Axis, etc.
    Which is the best RuPay credit card for movies? +
    Axis My Zone and Axis Neo are some of the best RuPay Credit Cards for movies as they offer benefits with BookMyShow, PVR, Distric App, etc. Apart from these, RuPay Credit Cards that offer higher value-back across all spends can be a good option to save on movies, especially for those who do not want to limit their benefits to select brands/platforms.
